اختبار تحديد المستوى

1 / 15
السؤال 1She ___ to school every day.
She goes to school every day.
السؤال 2I ___ my homework yesterday.
Yesterday signals the past: did.
السؤال 3There ___ two books on the table.
Two books = plural, so are.
السؤال 4He is ___ than his brother.
The comparative form is taller.
السؤال 5If I have time, I ___ you.
First conditional: If + present, will + base verb.
السؤال 6She speaks English ___.
Well is the adverb modifying speaks.
السؤال 7I have lived here ___ 2022.
Since is used with a starting point.
السؤال 8The opposite of 'cheap' is ___.
Cheap ↔ expensive.
السؤال 9They ___ watching TV now.
They are watching = present continuous.
السؤال 10By the time we arrived, the film ___.
Past perfect shows the earlier past action.
السؤال 11I ___ never been to London.
I have never been...
السؤال 12My brother is interested ___ football.
The fixed expression is interested in.
السؤال 13She has worked here ___ five years.
For is used with a period of time.
السؤال 14When I was young, I ___ football every Friday.
A finished repeated habit in the past uses played.
السؤال 15If I ___ enough money, I would buy a new phone.
Second conditional: If + past simple, would + base verb.
